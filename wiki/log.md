# Wiki Update Log

Newest first. One entry per change to the bundle's shape, not per typo fix.

* **Creation**: Added [`references/`](references/index.md), OKF's own convention (§6) for external material: [OKF v0.2](references/okf.md) summarises the specification and records every local decision taken against it, and [LLM Wiki](references/llm-wiki.md) names the organising pattern while stating plainly that it is not a published standard. Both carry `generated` and no `verified`, so they read as `unverified` until a person reviews them.
* **Ingestion**: Pulled the repo's remaining first-party engineering documentation into the bundle — 21 files from `docs/`, `.plans/`, `deploy/vps-hardened/` and `.devmind/product/quality/security/`, each gaining OKF frontmatter and landing in `operations/`, `state/`, `surfaces/`, `extensions/`, `decisions/` or `research/`. Deliberately **not** ingested, with reasons: `website/` (a published Docusaurus site), `skills/` and `optional-skills/` (executable content that `build_skills_index.py` walks at runtime), `.security/` (gitignored, generated, largely third-party), `AGENTS.md` and the root governance files (the wiki must never hold a copy of a rule), and three untracked `.devmind` assessments that exist as `.md`/`.json` pairs.
* **Ingestion**: `gateway/platforms/ADDING_A_PLATFORM.md` became [Adding a messaging platform](surfaces/adding-a-platform.md). It was the last first-party file that documented a subsystem rather than orienting someone standing in a folder, and it was giving a second answer to a question `index.md` already routed to `surfaces/gateway.md`. `docker/SOUL.md` was assessed and deliberately left in place: it is not documentation but the container's seeded system prompt (`docker/stage2-hook.sh` line 408).
