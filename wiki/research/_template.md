# NNNN. The question, phrased as a question

## Why this was asked

The decision or blocker waiting on the answer. If nothing was waiting, say so; curiosity
is a valid reason and it changes how much the conclusion should be trusted.

## Method

How the evidence was gathered, in enough detail that someone could repeat it and get
the same numbers. Name versions, dates, hardware, sample sizes. An unrepeatable
benchmark is an anecdote.

## Findings

The evidence. Numbers in tables, claims attributed to their source with a footnote so
each one can be traced.[^some-source]

[^some-source]: What this source actually said, and where.

## Conclusion

The answer to the question at the top, stated without hedging. Where the evidence was
not strong enough to conclude, say that plainly instead of dressing up a guess.

## What this does not cover

The adjacent questions this investigation deliberately left alone, so the next person
knows where the map ends.
